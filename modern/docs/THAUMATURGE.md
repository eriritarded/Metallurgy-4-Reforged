# Thaumaturge compatibility (NeoForge 26.1.2)

Thaumaturge is published only for Minecraft 26.1.2 / NeoForge (CurseForge project 1628024, 1.0.1 and
1.0.2, checked 2026-11-01), so this compat exists only in `neoforge-26.1.2`. It is optional: every
piece is data that Thaumaturge reads only when it is installed, or a recipe guarded by
`neoforge:mod_loaded`. Metallurgy loads the same without it.

The API facts below were read from `thaumaturge-26.1.2-NeoForge-1.0.2.jar`.

## Config

In `config/metallurgy_reforged/general`:

- `Thaumaturge Integration` (default true): every entry and recipe below.
- `Unify Metals Shared With Thaumaturge` (default true): when both mods are installed, a metal both
  of them have is made only Thaumaturge's way.

Both are read through `IntegrationFlags` (`thaumaturge`, `thaumaturge_unify`) by the existing
`metallurgy:integration_enabled` condition.

## Done

All of this is generated by `tools/generate_thaumaturge_data.py`; edit the tables there, not the JSON.

- **Aspects** (`data/thaumaturge/data_maps/item/base_aspects.json`): ingot, nugget, dust and block for
  all 49 metals, ore tags, and the non-metal resources (sulfur, phosphorus, potash, bitumen, tar,
  thermite). Each metal has Metallum by tier plus a theme aspect. Nuggets are 1/7 of an ingot,
  dusts add Perditio 1, blocks are 3x plus Ordo 5, and ores add Terra 5, matching Thaumaturge's own
  vanilla ratios. Tools, armour and decorative blocks get their aspects from Thaumaturge's
  crafting-recipe scan. Alloys are listed by hand, because that scan cannot see the alloyer. Ore
  tags Thaumaturge already maps (copper, iron, gold) are skipped, because duplicate keys would clash.
  NeoForge data maps take conditions per entry, so each entry carries the integration condition.
- **Infernal Furnace bonus** (`infernal_bonus.json`): 33% chance of 2 nuggets for each Metallurgy ore,
  as Thaumaturge does for vanilla ores. Silver, tin and copper are left to Thaumaturge's own entries.
- **Brass unify**: both mods' brass ingots share `c:ingots/brass`. When unified, Metallurgy's
  copper + zinc alloyer recipe and its brass-dust smelting are switched off, and brass dust smelts
  into `thaumaturge:ingot_brass` (`recipe/compat/thaumaturge/brass_dust_smelting.json`). This
  leaves Thaumaturge's crucible brass as the only brass made. Metallurgy brass items still accept
  either ingot through the tag.

## Content (1.4.1-port.21)

Registered in `compat/thaumaturge/ThaumaturgeCompat.java` only when Thaumaturge is installed. Models, recipes,
research, tags and lang come from `tools/generate_thaumaturge_content.py <Thaumaturge's data/thaumaturge/recipe>`;
cluster and gadget textures from `tools/generate_thaumaturge_art.py <textures dir>`. Recipes carry the integration
condition. Effect numbers are data: `data/metallurgy/thaumaturge/tuning.json` (a datapack can override it).

- **Wand caps** (arcane workbench, five nuggets): bronze 1.05, steel 1.00, platinum 0.95; mithril 0.92, orichalcum
  0.90 and adamantine 0.85 are made inert and charged by infusion. Elemental caps cost 1.00 but 20% less of one
  primal: Ignatius Ignis, Ceruclase Aqua, Shadow Steel Perditio, Sanguinite Terra, Midasium Ordo, Etherium Aer
  (caps can only discount vis, and vis is primal, so each metal's theme maps onto the nearest primal). Strange caps
  change each cast through `SpellCastEvent.Pre`: quicksilver, kalendrite, astral silver, tartarite, krik,
  amordrine, desichalkos, prometheum.
- **Rods**: Vulcanite (75 wand / 175 staff, glows, refills Ignis like blaze) and Eximite (100 / 200; Alienis is not
  a vis type, so it tops up a random low primal, three times as fast in the End).
- **Native clusters** for 28 ores: crucible on `#c:ores/<metal>` (Metallum 5, Ordo 5, research
  `thaumaturge:metal_purification`), smelt or blast to 2 ingots, Crusher to 3 dusts.
- **Gadgets**: Thaumium Knuckles, Thaumium Buckler, Void Gauntlet, Void Buckler (arcane workbench). They reuse the
  brass knuckles, rubracium gauntlet and zinc buckler behaviour; the thaumium/void weapons hit one point harder and
  the void ones mend themselves.
- **Etherium Goggles** reveal like Goggles of Revealing and take 5% off vis costs; with Thaumaturge their recipe
  needs Goggles of Revealing. Astral Silver, Etherium, Mithril and Amordrine armour take 1 to 3% off per piece
  (the `thaumaturge:vis_discount` attribute).
- **Research tab** "Metallurgy" (`data/metallurgy/thaumaturge/research_category`, `research_entry`); scanning any
  Metallurgy ore (`#metallurgy:thaumaturge_scan_ores`) opens it.
- **Recipe swaps**: the built-in pack `resourcepacks/thaumaturge` sits above Thaumaturge and replaces six of its
  recipes, each with its own config line (`Thaumaturge Recipe Swap: ...`): Fancy Amulet and Girdle take Midasium,
  Fancy Ring Platinum, Recharge Pedestal Orichalcum, Bellows and Golem Fetter Steel.
- **Quicksilver unify** (with the unify config): Thaumaturge's nugget is the nugget. Nine nuggets make Metallurgy's
  ingot and the ingot makes nine Thaumaturge nuggets; Metallurgy's own nugget recipe, the silver + astral silver
  alloy and Thaumaturge's nugget-to-gem recipe are off, and old Metallurgy nuggets convert one to one.

## Building

The 26.1.2 build needs `-PthaumaturgeJar=<thaumaturge-26.1.2-NeoForge-1.1.0.jar>` (compile only, nothing bundled),
like `-PhephaestusToolsJar`.
